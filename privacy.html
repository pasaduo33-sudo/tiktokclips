Privacy Policy - Clips TikTok personal

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Overview

Clips TikTok personal is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It has no other users.

2. Information we access

Through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token for the owner's own account, with the permissions user.info.basic and video.publish. These are used only to upload videos to that account.

3. Storage

The access token is stored locally on the owner's personal computer. It is not sent to, sold to or shared with any third party, and it is not stored on any external server.

4. Data collection

The app does not collect, track or process personal data from any other person.

5. Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok's account settings, and can delete the locally stored token.
